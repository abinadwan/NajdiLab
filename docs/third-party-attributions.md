## Required notices versus acknowledgments

When actual copies/derivatives are distributed, satisfy each applicable license's conditions. Apache-2.0 requires the license, relevant retained notices, marked modifications and applicable NOTICE attribution in permitted locations; MIT/BSD preserve specified notices/disclaimers, with BSD's non-endorsement condition. Framework licenses do not license weights, tokenizers, templates, datasets or personal records. Exact-artifact review includes native/transitive binaries and copied snippets, not just top-level imports.

A voluntary acknowledgment can thank external projects even where code is merely separately installed or referenced; it does not replace legal notices or imply endorsement. NajdiLab's original repository credit remains proposed in [license approval package](proposals/licensing-approval.md). No universal homepage-backlink obligation is asserted. Neither a root LICENSE nor a final active NOTICE is added.

Before any distribution, build the precise allowlisted source/binary manifest, resolve unknown copying/assets, collect exact upstream notice files and permissions, and recheck [public release decision](public-release-decision.md). Keep private research and operational assets excluded by default. This table does not close those gates.
