# Community conduct

Be respectful, constructive and evidence-focused. Welcome people with different backgrounds and experience. Explain disagreements clearly; do not harass, threaten, discriminate, expose private information or publish other people's conversations/credentials.

Maintainers may request corrections, remove harmful material or limit participation after reviewing context. Report concerns privately through a maintainer-approved channel. If none is configured, request a private channel without posting sensitive details. No reporting email or response-time commitment has been established yet. Before public launch, designate a responsible maintainer and private reporting route.

This is project-authored guidance, not affiliation with an external conduct program. Security reports follow SECURITY.md.
